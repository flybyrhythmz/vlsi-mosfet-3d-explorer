VLSI Engineering Knowledge Base

1. MOSFET 3 Operating Regions

  • Cutoff (VGS < Vth): Channel absent. Zero drift current, only tiny subthreshold leakage.
  • Triode/Linear (VGS > Vth, VDS < Vov): Inversion channel is continuous from source to drain. Functions as voltage-controlled resistor.
  • Saturation (VGS > Vth, VDS ≥ Vov): Channel pinches off at drain edge. Current saturates and is controlled solely by VGS.

2. Short-Channel Effects (SCE)

As channel length L scales below 100nm, planar MOSFETs encounter DIBL (Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering), velocity saturation, and gate oxide quantum tunneling. This spurred the revolution to 3D FinFETs and Gate-All-Around (GAA) Nanosheets!
